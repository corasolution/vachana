Privacy Policy

វចនា (Vachana) — Khmer Dictionary · Last updated 6 October 2026

In short: វចនា has no accounts and no sign-up. We do not collect your name, email, phone number or location. Your saved words and quiz progress never leave your phone. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which uses an advertising identifier.

Who we are

វចនា is published by Cora Solution (www.corasolution.com). This policy explains what the app does with information when you use it.

What stays on your device

The dictionary works offline. The following is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone, is never uploaded to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the app:

  • Words you bookmark
  • Your quiz answers and review schedule
  • Your recent searches
  • Which extra examples you have unlocked, and your daily quiz count

We have no server that stores user data, and no way to identify you.

What leaves your device

WhenWhat is sentWhere
Ads are shown Your device's advertising identifier and standard ad request data, handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK Google AdMob

The app checks for dictionary updates A request for an update file. As with any download, our content host sees the request and your IP address. No identifier is sent. Our content host

Nothing else is transmitted. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting or tracking software of any kind.

Advertising

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to show and measure ads, including personalised ads where you have consented. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

In regions where it is required, the app asks for your advertising consent the first time you open it, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your Android settings, under Settings → Google → Ads.

Children

វចនា is intended for students and adults aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Delete everything: uninstalling the app removes all of your saved words, quiz progress and settings. There is no account to close, because none is created.
  • Advertising: reset or limit your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Stay fully offline: the dictionary works with no internet connection. With no connection, nothing at all leaves your device.

Dictionary sources

The app's content comes from published reference works and open datasets. Every source and its licence is listed on the About screen inside the app.
